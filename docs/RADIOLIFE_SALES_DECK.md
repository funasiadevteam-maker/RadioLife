# RadioLife — Sales & Marketing Deck (15 Slides)
### Talking-point pointers for the Sales & Marketing team
*Focus: key platform features + what each one achieves for businesses.*

> Positioning line to lead every pitch:
> **"RadioLife is the social-commerce layer for local business — it turns listening, community and rewards into foot traffic and sales."**

---

## SLIDE 1 — RadioLife: Do Things Together, Locally
- The consumer app for **community commerce**: audio + local businesses + deals + payments + rewards, all connected.
- Not another social feed — it answers *"what are my friends and community doing together right now?"*
- One connected activity graph: **People · Audio · Businesses · Places · Products · Events · Games · Rewards.**
- **For business:** you're not buying an ad slot — you're plugged into what customers already do every day.

---

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem We Solve for Local Business
- Local merchants overpay for ads that don't convert and can't prove ROI.
- Loyalty programs are siloed; social reach is rented from platforms that don't send real customers.
- Discovery is broken: great local businesses are invisible next to national chains.
- **What RadioLife achieves:** measurable, merchant-funded engagement that drives real visits, orders and repeat customers — with attribution you can see.

---

## SLIDE 3 — What RadioLife Is (the Stack)
- **Listen:** live radio, AI radio, podcasts, news — always-on audio that keeps users in the app.
- **Local:** nearby businesses, storefronts, menus, offers, events.
- **Commerce:** catalog, checkout, gift cards, Dining Club, redemption.
- **Play & Earn:** games, BigPoints rewards, referrals.
- **Social V2:** Listen Together, Live Commerce, Moments, Clubs, Go Together.
- **For business:** every layer is a new, low-friction way for customers to find, engage and buy from you.

---

## SLIDE 4 — The Hook: Soft Launch = 6 Months Free
- Onboarding merchants get **6 months free** during Business Setup Mode.
- Guided **Business Setup** with a live **Launch Readiness %** so owners finish setup fast.
- **Preview My Store** + client preview links to demo before going public.
- **For business:** zero-risk entry, fully set up and "launch-ready" before the consumer app opens — first movers own their category locally.
- **Sales angle:** "Claim your spot free for 6 months before your competitor does."

---

## SLIDE 5 — Merchant Portal & Roles
- Premium dashboard: sales, redemptions, followers, engagement — one place.
- **Role-based staff access:** Owner, Manager, Cashier, Marketing, Analytics — invite your team safely.
- **Workspace switching:** owners with multiple stores switch instantly, no re-login.
- Works across every design variant; one shared, secure backend.
- **For business:** run the whole operation and delegate to staff without handing over the keys.

---

## SLIDE 6 — 🎥 RadioLife LIVE (Flagship Merchant Feature)
- **Go-Live wizard:** Product Sale · Restaurant/Chef Live · Service Demo · Flash Sale · Launch · Ask-the-Expert.
- **Pin your existing products/menu/offers** — live viewers add to cart and check out without leaving the stream.
- Live chat, reactions, **limited-time offers + 2× BigPoints**, then **replay & AI highlight clips** that stay shoppable.
- Reuses your real catalog, pricing, inventory, Stripe — no duplicate setup.
- **For business:** QVC-style live selling for local merchants — turn attention into orders in real time, then keep selling on the replay.

---

## SLIDE 7 — Social V2: "Do Things Together"
- **Listen Together** rooms: friends tune into the same station/podcast with live chat, reactions and shared moments.
- Together home shows live activity, friends online, and "do something together" — not an endless feed.
- Audio never stops as users browse, shop and socialize.
- **For business:** your live sessions, offers and storefront surface right inside these shared moments where groups make plans and spend together.

---

## SLIDE 8 — Moments: Social That Drives Visits
- Contextual photo/video/voice posts that **attach a station, dish, product, place or event** — each with the right CTA (**View Business / Shop / Play / Listen**).
- "Trying this at [your restaurant]" becomes a tappable path straight to your menu.
- Voice-first replies and reactions keep it authentic, not spammy.
- **For business:** every customer post is free, high-intent word-of-mouth with a direct link to buy or visit.

---

## SLIDE 9 — Clubs & Go Together: Groups = Bigger Orders
- **Clubs:** local interest communities (Foodies, Bollywood, Cricket, Brunch) — a warm audience you can sponsor and go live to.
- **Go Together:** group plans for dinners, events and outings with Going/Maybe/Decline and a shared plan page → directions, menu, **group order**, reservation.
- **For business:** capture the whole table, not one diner — group plans convert into group carts and reservations.

---

## SLIDE 10 — BigPoints: Merchant-Funded Loyalty
- A single, fraud-safe rewards ledger across the whole platform.
- Challenges can be **platform-, merchant- or sponsor-funded**, with tracked budget, issuance, redemption and expiry.
- Rewards for real actions: visits, purchases, event attendance, referrals — never for spam.
- **For business:** you fund exactly the behavior you want (repeat visits, live attendance, referrals) and see the liability and ROI clearly.

---

## SLIDE 11 — Games & Engagement
- Merchant games: **Scratch & Win, Spin & Win**, plus Trivia and Predict & Win (no wagering).
- Team play, challenges and leaderboards make engagement social and sticky.
- Prizes = your coupons, % off, or gift cards — all on your terms.
- **For business:** a playful, low-cost way to pull in new customers and reward loyal ones, with built-in redemption tracking.

---

## SLIDE 12 — Dining Club, Redemption & Cashier POS
- **Dining Club** memberships and fair, tamper-proof restaurant selection (sponsorship never changes the odds).
- **Cashier POS:** big Scan-QR / Enter-Code screen → validate → redeem → success, with **today's redemption totals** for end-of-day reconciliation.
- PIN-gated staff redemption + audit trail.
- **For business:** frictionless in-store redemption your staff can run in seconds, with clean daily numbers.

---

## SLIDE 13 — Payments, Gift Cards & Attribution
- Built on **Stripe** with gift cards, prepaid meals, offers and memberships — one commerce engine, no bolt-ons.
- **Won/earned gift cards are spendable at your counter**, driving guaranteed return visits.
- Analytics: unique viewers, product clicks, add-to-cart, purchases, offers claimed, new followers, repeat customers.
- **For business:** real revenue attribution — see which live session, offer or post actually drove the sale.

---

## SLIDE 14 — 🔮 Dreams & Guidance: Services Marketplace (New)
- A distinct reflective community: share dreams, AI reflections, **verified advisors** (astrology, tarot, numerology, dream interpretation), and **paid readings/consultations**.
- Advisors are onboarded as service providers reusing the same identity, **Stripe payouts, scheduling, reviews and marketplace** foundations.
- Clear guardrails: entertainment/reflection positioning, verified-credential badges, respectful (never fear-based) upsell.
- **For business/advisors:** a new paid-services revenue stream — bookings, live workshops and consultations — on rails you already trust.

---

## SLIDE 15 — Why It Works & Call to Action
- **The moat:** Audio + real-time social + local businesses + live commerce + games + BigPoints + real-world experiences — connected, not siloed.
- **What businesses get:** cheaper reach, measurable conversions, merchant-funded loyalty, live selling, group orders and a services marketplace — all in one platform.
- **Metrics that matter:** live viewers → clicks → carts → sales, redemptions, repeat visits, followers, BigPoints ROI.
- **Call to action:** *"Claim your free 6-month launch, set up your storefront, and go live to your community before your competitors do."*
